# Cat Run activities

While a timer is running, the menu-bar cat randomly chooses from these 20 activities:

1. **Tail swish:** sweeps its tail from side to side.
2. **Look around:** turns its head and shifts its gaze.
3. **Paw wave:** raises a forepaw.
4. **Happy hop:** makes a small upward hop.
5. **Blink and ear twitch:** closes its eyes as its ears wiggle.
6. **Long stretch:** reaches forward with its tail raised.
7. **Walk:** takes alternating steps in side profile.
8. **Trot:** takes quicker steps with a bounce.
9. **Curl up:** curls into a sleepy ball.
10. **Roll over:** makes a playful tumble.
11. **Groom:** licks a raised paw.
12. **Ear scratch:** scratches beside an ear with a hind paw.
13. **Shake:** wiggles its body.
14. **Yawn:** closes its eyes and opens its mouth.
15. **Sniff:** leans toward a small flower.
16. **Pounce:** springs forward with reaching paws.
17. **Ball play:** follows a bouncing striped ball with its paw.
18. **Butterfly watch:** watches a fluttering butterfly.
19. **Heart purr:** wears a content expression beside a pulsing heart.
20. **Peek:** peeks over a box with its paws on the rim.

Each activity lasts about two seconds. A new activity starts every five seconds, without immediately repeating the previous choice. Selection is random, so a session may not show every activity.

These animations are silent. The cat stays still when the timer is inactive. Low Power Mode uses gentler animation; Reduce Motion, a sleeping display, or serious thermal pressure suppresses movement. The header uses the same sitting cat artwork as the idle menu-bar icon and remains static.
